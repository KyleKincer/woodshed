# Woodshed

Woodshed is a personal music library for practicing along with songs and their separated stems.

## Language

**Song metadata**:
The identifying and descriptive details attached to a song in a person's Woodshed library, including title, artist, album, artwork, and organizational labels.

**Practice information**:
Details attached to a library song to support practicing it, such as musical key, tuning, and personal notes.

**Metadata enrichment**:
Automatic identification and completion of a library song's descriptive details using internet sources, with uncertain matches left for optional review and manual corrections preserved.

**Recording**:
A particular recorded performance of a song, distinct from other performances, live versions, or remixes. The same recording can appear on multiple releases.

**Release**:
A particular issued album, single, or compilation containing recordings. It provides the context for album details and cover artwork.
